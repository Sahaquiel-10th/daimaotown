# 01：风格锚点与冒险家公会

## A1. 项目工坊风格锚点

本次不要上传任何参考图。直接完整复制下面这段：

```text
Create one isolated building asset for an isometric web game.

The building is a low, wide community project workshop with a simple charcoal roof, warm cream walls, a broad open doorway, two square windows, a blank notice board, and one small warm-yellow (#FFD76B) awning.

Visual style: clean 2D digital cut-paper collage, with a few layered paper shapes, subtle paper grain, simple charcoal ink details, and crisp readable edges. It should look illustrated, not photographed.

Camera: orthographic isometric three-quarter top view. Show the complete roof and the complete base of the building.

Place only this one building in the center of a plain warm light-gray background, with generous empty space around it.

No people, no cats, no trees, no road, no scenery, no ground island, no text, no logo, no cast shadow. Do not make it look like a physical miniature, a house, a church, a castle, or a fantasy shop.
```

建议连续生成 2～4 次，从中选一张。不要在第一次结果上不断追加很多修改。

## A2. 冒险家公会

先上传已经选定的项目工坊图片，然后完整复制下面这段：

```text
Use the uploaded project-workshop image as the visual style reference.

Create one isolated central adventurers' guild building for the same isometric web game.

The guild is a low, wide civic hall with a large welcoming entrance and two horizontal wings. The left wing represents project commissions and the right wing represents skill services. Add a blank notice-board area near the entrance, but do not draw any words.

Keep the same 2D digital cut-paper collage style, paper texture, camera angle, lighting, edge treatment, and color restraint as the reference image. Warm cream and charcoal should dominate, with only small warm-yellow (#FFD76B) and pale-green (#BFE7C0) accents.

Show the whole building in orthographic isometric three-quarter top view. Center it on a plain warm light-gray background with generous empty space.

No people, no cats, no trees, no road, no scenery, no text, no logo, no cast shadow. The silhouette must be horizontal and civic. Do not add a tower, spire, circular church window, castle wall, or palace dome.
```

## A3. 如果公会还是像教堂

上传刚生成的公会图，只复制这一段进行单点修改：

```text
Keep the paper-collage style, camera angle, colors, windows, and entrance unchanged.

Change only the overall silhouette: make the building lower and much wider, with two clear horizontal civic wings. Remove every church-like, cathedral-like, castle-like, tower-like, or palace-like feature.

Do not change anything else.
```
